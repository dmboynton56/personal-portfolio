# Portfolio Analytics FAQ

## What does "edge" mean here?
`edge_pts` is the model spread minus the book spread. It measures disagreement size, not guaranteed value.

## Why can edge direction feel counterintuitive?
Spreads use home-team perspective. Sign interpretation depends on whether the home team is favored or an underdog in each line.

## Why might the UI and chat disagree at times?
They can use different windows or timing. Chat may query by explicit week, while cards may show a moving time window.

## Is a high spread hit rate proof of long-term performance?
No. Hit rate is one diagnostic. You should also monitor sample size, regime changes, and version-specific drift.

## What happens when Supabase is unavailable?
The Sports Edge API can fall back to mock payloads for UI continuity; chat will report missing SQL results and continue with docs-based context.

## What documentation should be kept current?
- Data dictionary
- Metric definitions
- Model cards
- Weekly postmortems
- Limitations/risk notes

## How should users phrase strong chat questions?
Best pattern: include league, time slice, and intent.

Examples:
- "NFL week 14 top deltas and brief rationale"
- "Explain edge_pts and spreadHit with formulas"
- "What risk caveats apply to this week's board?"
