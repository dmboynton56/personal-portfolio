# Model Card: NBA Hall of Fame Predictor (XGBoost)

## Purpose
Estimate Hall of Fame probability for NBA players using historical career outcomes and performance/accolade features.

## Decision Output
- `hofProbability` between 0 and 1
- Confidence bands (`Very High`, `High`, `Medium`, `Low`, `Very Low`)

## Dataset Scope
- Player pool represented in `public/data/nba_hof_predictions.json`
- Includes active and retired players
- Portfolio UI references this dataset for lookup and interactive analysis

## Feature Families
- Career totals (points, rebounds, assists, blocks)
- Career impact (`careerWinShares`, `careerVORP`)
- Peak metrics (`peakPER`, `peakBPM`, `peakVORP`, `peakWinShares`)
- Accolades (All-Star, All-NBA, MVP, DPOY, All-Defense)
- Longevity (`seasons`, last season indicators)

## Training Notes
- Gradient boosted trees (XGBoost)
- Structured tabular features with nonlinear interactions
- Output calibrated to produce interpretable probability style scores

## Intended Use
- Exploratory and educational player comparisons
- Demonstration of model-driven ranking and explanation in UI

## Non-Goals
- Official Hall of Fame selection forecasting
- Causal claims about voter behavior

## Known Limitations
- Era effects and selection criteria can drift
- International and non-NBA context may be underrepresented
- Dataset quality and labeling assumptions directly affect output quality
