# About Drew Boynton

## Summary

Drew is a developer who looks at data, models the events behind it, and
brings the results to life through web applications. His work spans
machine learning, software engineering, and product surface design —
the full path from raw data to something a person can actually use.

## Current role

Drew is an apprentice in the **Flatiron School Accelerated AI
Engineering Immersive** program. Day-to-day, the work looks like:

- Learning the capabilities of AI in code
- Applying those concepts to web app projects, both independently
  and in collaboration with his apprenticeship cohort
- Building production systems that exercise the full ML + web stack

The apprenticeship project's name is **MatchPoint** — a web app
maintained as a learning artifact within the cohort.

## What he's looking for

Drew is open to full-time opportunities as a:

- **Machine learning engineer**, or
- **Software engineer** with an ML or data emphasis

He's targeting roles where data and modeling are core to the product
(rather than ML-as-a-feature on a non-data product). He's open to
relocating for the right role, and would consider moving back to
Boston or to Austin, San Francisco, Chicago, or New York City.
Industry and company size are flexible; remote/hybrid/on-site are all
on the table.
