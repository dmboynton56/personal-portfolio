# Drew's story

## Where he grew up

Drew grew up just north of Boston, in Andover, Massachusetts.

## High school and the pivot to Colorado

In high school, Drew took a few computer science classes. After
graduating, he went skiing in Colorado, and the experience shaped his
next move: he decided to major in computer science at the University of
Colorado Boulder.

## Finding data science and ML

At CU Boulder, Drew got heavily into data science, data modeling, and
the machine learning processes that turn raw data into decisions. That
interest has carried through to the systems he builds today.

## Where he is now

Drew currently lives in Denver, Colorado, and is working to land his
first career position in the tech industry — specifically in software
engineering or machine learning.

He is open to relocating for the right role, and would consider moving
back to Boston or to Austin, San Francisco, Chicago, or New York City.

## How this story maps to the projects

The path from "a few CS classes in high school" to "production ML
pipelines with scheduled runs and serving tables" is the path the
portfolio projects document in technical detail. For the underlying
arc — the "why" behind the systems — this doc is the anchor.
